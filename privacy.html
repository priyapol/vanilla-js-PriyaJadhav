Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy describes what information the Rainy Days demonstration website handles and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

The shopping cart is saved in your browser's local storage so it remains available while you use the site. It contains product identifiers, names, prices, image references, and quantities. This demonstration site does not send cart contents to a payment or order-processing service.

Checkout information

The checkout form is for demonstration only. It does not submit your name, email address, or delivery address to a server, and no real order or payment is made. Please do not enter sensitive or real personal information.

Product data and external services

Product listings are requested from the Noroff Rainy Days API. Your browser connects directly to that service to retrieve product data. Social media links lead to third-party websites, which have their own privacy practices.

Cookies and tracking

This demonstration website does not intentionally use analytics or advertising trackers. Your browser and third-party services may handle technical data when you access the site or follow an external link.

Your choices

You can remove saved cart data by clearing this site's local storage through your browser settings. You can avoid submitting information to third-party websites by not following their links.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the demonstration site, contact hello@rainydays.com.
